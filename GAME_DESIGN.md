# Apocalypse Barista — Game Concept

## 1. Overview

Apocalypse Barista is a comedy action-adventure game with role-playing elements.
The player is a barista in a world where almost all people fell asleep.
Coffee keeps people awake.
The player brews coffee and uses it to wake the sleepwalkers.
The mission is to cure the whole United States of Americano, one city at a time.

## 2. Story

The story is one chain of cause and effect:
The extract is the cause. The address is the trigger. Real coffee is the cure.

Mug Roasters Co. is the largest coffee corporation in America.
The corporation made a synthetic caffeine extract.
Synthetic caffeine is cheap. The corporation put it into almost every drink in the United States of Americano.
People drank it to work more and sleep less.
The corporation slogan was "Join The Grind".
Extraction leaves empty beans behind.
The corporation sold the empty rest as decaf coffee.
Decaf looks like coffee, but it has no caffeine.
Decaf was the status drink of the political elite in the capital.
The capital drank decaf for years.

But the body stores all missed sleep. The stored sleep is the sleep debt.
The sleep debt grows with every short night.

One scientist at Mug Roasters Co. discovered the sleep debt.
The scientist warned the corporation. The corporation did not listen.
The scientist recorded a video message.
The message says: "I did a very bad thing. Now I leave it to you to correct it."

Then came the address night.
President Roland Puck gave a special address on television.
Roland Puck is a comedy figure, a mix of American presidents.
All channels showed the address. The whole country watched.
The address was extremely boring. The nation fell asleep.
Decaf City fell asleep first, and it fell deepest.
The president drank only decaf.
President Puck fell asleep in the middle of his own address.
The emergency broadcast system switched to automatic repeat.
No awake person could stop it.
The address still plays, on every screen and every speaker, day and night.
The nation cannot wake up for good while the address runs.
President Puck sleepwalks through his own address, on repeat. He is the source of the loop.

A normal nap ends in the morning.
A sleep-debt nap never ends. The body collects all the missed sleep at once.
The nation did not wake up. The nation started to sleepwalk.

Only one group stayed awake: the people who drank real brewed coffee during the address.
Natural caffeine is the shield against the sleep debt.
Real coffee is also the only cure for the sleepwalkers.

People who missed the address crashed in the days after, at their next sleep.
The sleepwalking covers only the United States of Americano. Mug Roasters Co. sold the extract only there.

At game start, the player wakes up in the café.
The video of the scientist plays on the café screen.
The game begins.

## 3. World and setting

The country name and the national symbols:

- Country name: the United States of Americano.
- Citizens: Americanos. The player turns sleeping Americanos into awake Americanos.
- Flag: the "Stars and Crema".
- National anthem: the "Star-Blooming Banner".
- Uncle Steam: the man on the old corporation posters. The posters say: "WE WANT YOU to join the grind."

The map of the United States of Americano uses parody city names.
The game starts in San Coffesco (San Francisco).
The player travels east.
The final mission takes place in Decaf City (Washington D.C.).

City names:

| Real city | Game name |
| --- | --- |
| San Francisco | San Coffesco |
| Seattle | Steamtle |
| Portland | Pourtland |
| Chicago | Brewcago |
| St. Louis | St. Brewis |
| Houston | Brewston |
| Las Vegas | Latte Vegas |
| New Orleans | New Orbeans |
| New York | New Roast City, "The Big Bean" |
| Washington D.C. | Decaf City |

The Mug Roasters Co. tower stands in New Roast City.
Release plan: version 1 contains three cities: San Coffesco, Steamtle, and Pourtland.
The other cities come after version 1.
Decaf City is the only city with no real coffee. The address came from there.
The elite of the city drank decaf as a status symbol, and decaf gave no shield.
The city fell asleep first and sleeps deepest.
The few old real-coffee houses in the city are the only safe zones for the player.

### Decaf City difficulty

Decaf City is the hardest level of the game:

- The citizens drank decaf for years. Their bodies have zero caffeine tolerance in reverse: no caffeine habit means no light wake-up. Simple filter coffee wakes nobody in Decaf City. Only full specialty cups work.
- The city sleeps deepest. More sleepwalkers walk there than anywhere else.
- The streets have no real coffee. The player cannot refill the supply outside the safe zones.
- The years of decaf left a caffeine hunger in every citizen. One cup is never enough. The Withdrawer rule applies to almost everyone (see section 6).
- Parts of the city radiate sleep. The player becomes tired in these areas and must move fast or reach a safe zone.

The player must brew a full stock before entry and hop from safe zone to safe zone.
Decaf City is a supply-run level: the coffee supply is the real enemy.
Each city has its own map, survivors, and side quests.
The world is a light parody of America.

## 4. Visual style and view

The game uses a top-down 2D view, like the old Zelda games.
The player walks through the world and enters houses.
The view is flat 2D, not 3D and not isometric.
The visual style points to a newer top-down Zelda game.
The art is cartoon-like, friendly, and funny.

Platform: Android first.
The game is built for touch from day one: a virtual stick and touch buttons.
Play sessions are short. One city block fits into one coffee break.
Desktop and console versions can come later.

## 5. The player character

The game starts with character creation.
All options are cosmetic only:

- Body type: male or female.
- Skin tone.
- Hair style.
- Beard length: clean, stubble, hipster, lumberjack, or wizard.
- Tattoos: number and placement.
- Clothing style: steampunk: hats, goggles, aprons, boots.

The beard is open to all body types.
This is a comedy game, not a passport office.

The player is a barista and owns a café.
At game start, the player has no equipment.
The player throws cups with coffee as the first weapon.

## 6. Sleepwalkers

The sleepwalkers are not classic zombies:

- They are pale people with their arms in front of them.
- They drool. The drool is exaggerated for comedy.
- Some sleepwalkers wet their pants. This is funny, not disgusting.
- They walk in their sleep.
- They eat everything they find, also coffee plants.

Rules for sleepwalkers:

- The player cannot kill them. Coffee is the only cure.
- A sleepwalker attacks with a hug.
- The sleepwalkers dream. In the dream, they look for a warm hug.
- The hug is so comfortable that the player becomes sleepy.
- If a sleepwalker holds the player for too long, the player falls asleep.
- The player must dodge the hugs.

Enemy type "The Karen" (working name):
The Karen is very fat and looks slow, but she moves very fast.
She gives the player a surprise attack.
Normal coffee does not wake her.
The Karen wakes up only from a super tall American coffee.
When she wakes up, she asks for the manager.

### More enemy types (working names)

Every enemy type has one special behavior and one special cure.

- The Tech Bro: he sleep-pitches startup ideas to nobody. A drone circles him and attacks the player. Cure: bulletproof coffee.
- The Gamer: he had the highest synthetic caffeine level in the country. He moves in fast, short dashes. Cure: a triple Aeropress shot.
- The Crossfitter: he is extremely fit and sleep-lifts park benches. He throws them far. Cure: a protein cold brew.
- The Tourist: he stops and takes photos of everything. The camera flash stuns the player for a moment. Cure: a Frappuccino with whipped cream.
- The Trucker: he walks slowly, but his CB radio horn calls a herd of sleepwalkers. Cure: one full pot of filter coffee.
- The Night Nurse: she hugs the people that the player just woke. They fall asleep and sleepwalk again. Cure: a double espresso.
- The Tinfoil Man: his tinfoil hat stops thrown coffee. Splash attacks do not work on him. Cure: Turkish coffee.
- The Executive: his sleep-talk sounds like a conference call. It summons all sleepwalkers in the area. Cure: a 12-dollar single-origin V60.
- The Withdrawer: a decaf drinker from Decaf City. One cup wakes him, but the wake does not hold. The player must give a second cup within one minute. Without the second cup, he sleeps again.

Easter egg enemy, The Moonwalker (working name):
The Moonwalker is very rare. Some players will say that he does not exist.
He wears one white glove, a red jacket, and white socks.
He never walks forward. He only slides backwards, and that is fast.
His moves give wrong signals: he looks like he goes away, but he comes closer.
He does not hug. He leans far back and slides under the guard of the player.
Cure: one ristretto, the smoothest shot in the game.
When he wakes up, he spins one time, stands on his toes, and makes a small "hee-hee".
The game uses no real name, no real likeness, and no real music for this enemy.

The enemy types follow the route:
Tech types live in San Coffesco. Suburban types live in the middle of the country. Political types live in Decaf City.

### Boss ideas (working names)

- Muggy: the Mug Roasters Co. mascot, a giant walking coffee mug. City boss. The player must brew him one perfect cup. Muggy tastes the cup, cries, and wakes up.
- The Senator: he sleep-holds speeches on the Senate floor. His speech is so boring that the player becomes sleepy inside his aura. Decaf City boss. Cure: a super large Americano.

Route bosses, in travel order:

- San Coffesco, Sam Oatman, "One More Thing": a keynote guru in a gray fleece vest. He sleep-presents a product show to a hall of sleepwalking founders. His slides are boring. They make the player sleepy. The live product demos send drones (see the Tech Bro). Every fight phase starts with the words "One more thing". He presents his coffee machine as the last product humanity will ever need. Cure: one cup from his own 700-dollar designer dripper, served on the demo table. When he wakes up, he presents the cup as his next product.
- Steamtle, The Fish Throwers: the fish sellers of the public market. They sleep-throw fish with perfect aim, in a steady rhythm. The player dodges the fish volley and wakes the head fishmonger first. They never miss, and they never drop a fish. Cure: one tall cup of the market dark roast, thrown into his apron. When the crew wakes up, they throw the fish one last time, all together, and cheer.
- Pourtland, The Sleep Jam: an indie band that sleep-performs one endless song. The song is a lullaby. It makes the player sleepy. Cure: a cup from the most obscure single-origin beans in the world.
- Brewcago, Second Snooze: an improv troupe from the famous comedy school. They sleep-perform one endless improv show for a hall of sleeping fans. Every fight phase is a new improv game with a new rule. In "Questions Only" they speak only questions, and the questions make the player tired. In "Freeze" they stop all moves at the same time and then dash together. In "New Choice" the stage furniture moves and the arena changes shape. Cure: the player serves one perfect cup as an audience suggestion. The troupe drinks, wakes up, and bows. The laughter wakes the whole hall. After the fight, they turn the apocalypse into their new hit show.
- Brewston, The Fry King: a state-fair cook with a giant deep fryer. He throws deep-fried coffee. Fried coffee wakes nobody. Cure: one honest pot of filter coffee.
- Latte Vegas, The Elvis Choir: one hundred sleepwalking Elvis impersonators. They sing "Can't Help Falling Asleep". Crowd fight. The caffeine cluster bomb works well here. Cure: one Elvis latte for the lead Elvis.
- New Orbeans, The Jazz Funeral: the whole city sleepwalks in one parade. A trumpet player plays a sleepy lullaby at the front. Cure: one chicory café au lait with a beignet.
- New Roast City, The Charging Bull: the Wall Street statue came to life. It charges in straight lines and gets stuck in walls. Cure: one espresso with gold flakes, price 29 dollars.
- New Roast City, Mug Roasters HQ, The CEO: he sleeps in a power-nap pod. His "synergy" speech summons employee hordes. Cure: one cup that contains all eight brewing methods. The last skill check before Decaf City. The laboratory of the scientist is in the HQ. The player learns the full story there.

## 7. Core gameplay loop

1. Explore the open world.
2. Collect coffee beans.
3. Scavenge gear and materials.
4. Return to the café.
5. Brew coffee.
6. Go out and wake sleepwalkers with coffee.
7. Switch off the broadcast tower of the city. Then the woken people stay awake.
8. Complete quests and unlock the next city.

The game has no money.
The player takes everything the player finds.
There are no shops, no daily orders, and no income.

## 8. Brewing

Brewing is the crafting system of the game:

- Brewing methods: filter coffee, Turkish coffee, French press, V60 pour-over, moka pot, espresso, Aeropress, cold brew.
- Different beans and different roast levels give different coffees.
- The sleepwalker types need different brewing methods (see sections 6 and 9).
- More special coffee wakes stronger sleepwalkers.
- Example: only a super tall American coffee wakes a Karen (see section 6).

The player brews through a minigame.
Good performance gives better coffee.
Repeated good brewing increases the brewing skill.
A high skill level removes the minigame requirement later in the game.

### The brewing minigames (proposals)

Every brew starts with the same pre-step:

- The Grind Dial: a marker runs across a scale from fine to coarse. The player stops the marker in the correct zone for the method. A wrong grind lowers the quality of the cup.

Then one minigame per method group:

- The Pour (V60 pour-over, filter coffee, Aeropress): the player pours in a slow circle. A "bloom ring" fills up. Pour too fast and it floods. Pour too slow and it stalls. The ring shows the sweet spot.
- The Press (espresso, French press, moka pot): a two-step hold. Step one: hold the pressure in the green zone while it creeps. Step two: release at the exact mark. Early means sour. Late means bitter.
- The Foam Watch (Turkish coffee): the pot heats up and the foam rises. More foam gives a better cup, but the pot can boil over. The player lifts the pot at the last safe moment. Greed costs the cup.
- Cold brew: no minigame. The player starts it and comes back later. Low ceiling, zero stress.

A good result gives skill points for the method (see section 14).
At method skill 10, the player can switch on auto-brew for that method.
Auto-brew always gives a good cup. It never gives a perfect cup.

### Brewing masters

The brewing masters are parody characters of famous coffee personalities.
All names are working names:

- James Offmug: an elegant British barista. He speaks slowly and says "Ultimately..." in every sentence. He teaches the V60 pour-over.
- Lance Hedrip: an enthusiastic engineer. He brews with scales, thermometers, and technical drawings. He teaches the Aeropress and the cold brew.
- Scott Raoast: a strict coffee consultant. He writes notes about every cup he drinks. He teaches the espresso and the filter coffee.

Each master lives in a different city.
A master teaches a method and increases the brewing skill faster than solo practice.
A master also gives the player new recipes.
A recipe is a fixed combination of bean, roast level, and method.
Some sleepwalker types wake only from a master recipe (see section 9).

### More masters (candidates)

- Morgan Espressoth: parody of Morgan Eckroth. The latte-art duel opponent. She teaches latte art and the French press.
- Dritan Alsteam: parody of Dritan Alsela. A showman. He teaches the moka pot with full flair. His catchphrase: "Espresso yourself!"
- The Trip Twins: parody of European Coffee Trip. They teach no method. They rate the café of the player. A good rating brings more survivors into the café.
- The Champion: parody of a world barista champion. He teaches the "competition cup". This cup is the cure for the CEO boss (see section 6).

Turkish coffee has no master yet.

### Cameo strategy

Every master NPC has one fixed role and one fixed lesson.
A swap from parody character to real person is then only a new skin and new voice lines.
Parody names need no permission. Real cameos need written permission.
A real cameo gives the game promotion: the personality tells their audience "I am in this game".
The audiences of coffee channels overlap strongly with the audience of indie games.
Priority contact list: James Hoffmann (largest reach), Morgan Eckroth (TikTok), Lance Hedrick (strong community), Dritan Alsela (Instagram), European Coffee Trip (café tourism).
The parodies stay friendly. The game shows no real name and no real likeness without permission.

## 9. Player status and interface

The interface shows:

- The sleepy meter. A full meter means awake. An empty meter means sleep.
- Coffee supply. Coffee works like mana.
- Number of brewed cups in hand.
- Inventory: beans, brewed coffee, gear, and materials.
- Remaining wake-ups.

### The sleepy meter

The sleepy meter drains slowly all the time. The address still plays everywhere, and it pulls the player down.
The meter drains faster near loudspeakers, in sleep zones, and during boring attacks like slides, speeches, and endless questions.
Hugs drain the meter fast.
The player drinks a brewed cup to fill the meter again.
Every cup the player drinks is a cup the player cannot throw.

### Lives and game modes

When the sleepy meter is empty, the player falls asleep on the spot.
The player wakes up in the café. This costs one wake-up.
The player has a limited number of wake-ups. Working setting: five.
When the last wake-up is gone, the player dies. The game ends.

Game modes:

- Normal mode: the wake-up system as written above.
- Hardcore mode: the player has no wake-ups. The first sleep ends the game.

### The smartphone

The player carries a smartphone.
The smartphone has one app: "SleepyDex" (working name).
The SleepyDex is a list of all sleepwalker types, like a Pokédex.
Each entry shows:

- Picture and name of the sleepwalker type.
- Status: found or not found.
- The coffee that wakes this type.

A new entry opens when the player meets a sleepwalker type for the first time.
Example: the Karen entry shows the super tall American coffee as her cure.
Rare types are not in the SleepyDex before the player finds them.
The SleepyDex shows no placeholder and no hint that a rare type exists.
Example: the Moonwalker (see section 6). His entry appears only after the player meets him.
A completed SleepyDex gives one reward: a troll doll keychain.
The keychain has no game effect. It gives no stats, no skill, and no secret.
It hangs on the mirror of the mobile café, forever.
The game says one line: "It is just a keychain."
It stays just a keychain.

## 10. Base and progression

The café is the home base of the player.
The player moves from city to city.
Each new city has a new café.
Each new café is bigger and better than the old café.
The player moves all belongings from the old café to the new café.

### The mobile café

The van is the second base of the player: a café on wheels.
The survivors in the first city give the van to the player.
The player upgrades the van step by step:

- Level 1, the van: transport for tools, gear, beans, and equipment.
- Level 2, the kitchen: a brewing station inside. The player can brew outside the city café.
- Level 3, the field café: tables, an awning, and a lamp. The player can park anywhere and open a small field base. Awakened survivors gather there and give small side quests.

The mobile café is also the moving-day tool: it carries the equipment from the old city café to the new one.
The mobile café is too wide for the old streets of Decaf City. The player can park it only at the city edge.

## 11. Weapons and tools

Coffee is health, mana, and ammunition at the same time.
Every shot uses coffee from the player supply.
Cure weapons deliver brewed coffee. Support tools control the sleepwalkers.
The player finds better weapons along the route.

Start weapon:

- The Cup: the player throws one cup of coffee. Single target. Free refill at the café.

Cure weapons:

- The Travel Mug: a sealed steel mug. The player throws it. It bounces from one sleepwalker to a second one, also around corners. Then it rolls back to the player. The lid keeps the coffee inside. Reusable.
- The Thermos: the player throws a full thermos flask. It breaks and splashes coffee in a wide area.
- The French Press: a pump-action weapon for close range. One pump loads, the next pump shoots a cone of coffee.
- The Moka Launcher: a steampunk launcher. It fires pressurized moka pots in a high arc. The coffee brews during the flight. Large area, few shots.
- The Coffee Minigun: a Nerf-style minigun with low recoil. It fires a continuous stream of coffee. It drains the supply very fast.
- The Auto-Drip Turret: a deployable drip tower. It drips coffee on every sleepwalker below it. Slow, but it works alone.
- The Caffeine Cluster Bomb: see section 12.

Support tools:

- The Tamper: a melee push tool. It staggers one sleepwalker. It wakes nobody.
- The Steam Wand: a melee cone of steam. With correct timing, it blocks a hug.
- The Portafilter: a shield. It blocks hugs. The player can bash with it.
- The Iced Sprayer: it sprays iced coffee. Iced coffee wakes nobody, but it gives brain freeze. Sleepwalkers slow down.
- The Bean Slick: the player drops coffee beans on the ground. Sleepwalkers slip on the beans.

## 12. Quests

Main quest:
Travel from San Coffesco to Decaf City and wake up the whole United States of Americano.
Every city has one broadcast tower. The tower repeats the address without end.
The player switches the tower off. Then the woken people of the city stay awake.
The last tower stands in Decaf City.
The last mission wakes President Puck.
He sleepwalks through his own address, on repeat.
The player brews him one perfect cup.
Puck wakes up and holds a second address.
The second address replaces the loop on every channel.
The second address is interesting. The United States of Americano wakes up.

Side quests:
Survivors give side quests to the player.

Chemist side quest ("Breaking Bad" parody):
The player finds a former chemistry teacher.
The teacher knows the Mug Roasters Co. extraction process.
He uses the same process on real coffee beans.
The result is super-concentrated real caffeine.
The reward is the caffeine cluster bomb.
The bomb holds many mini bombs inside one large cardboard box.
The player fires the box with a slingshot.
On impact, all mini bombs explode at the same time.
One explosion wakes all sleepwalkers in a large area.

## 13. Side quests (version 1)

Each version 1 city has one signature side quest.

- The Robotaxi Depot, San Coffesco: the driverless taxis still run. They pick up sleepwalkers and deliver them to the player. The routing engineer sleeps in the depot control room. His order is written like code: triple shot, oat cortado, milk at exactly 31 degrees. The player brews the order and wakes him. Reward: the fleet re-routes. The taxis work as fast travel inside the city.
- Sleepvana, Steamtle: the retired grunge band sleeps in three places: the greenhouse, the garage, and the sofa. Three members wake to three simple orders. The frontman wakes only to a full crowd. The player gathers awakened fans at the field café and leads them to the garage. Then the player brews the frontman order during the encore. Reward: one last concert. The concert is a city wake event: every sleepwalker in hearing range wakes. Their hit song: "Smells Like Bean Spirit".
- The Aisle 7, Pourtland: a woman gives the player a photo. Her husband sleeps in aisle 7 of the hardware store, between the screws and the sandpaper. He drank only Turkish coffee, all his life. The player finds him and brews one Turkish cup in front of him. He wakes, cries, and hugs his wife. This is the game's only sweet hug. Bonus: he is the Turkish master. He teaches the Turkish method and its recipes. The sleeper becomes the teacher.

## 14. Working numbers

These numbers are working settings for the prototype. The prototype decides the final values.

### Sleepy meter

- Meter: 0 to 100. New day: 100.
- Passive drain: 0.5 per second.
- Near loudspeakers and in sleep zones: 1.5 per second.
- During boring attacks, like slides and speeches: 5 per second.
- Hug: 15 per second while held.
- Drink one cup: +40. Drinking takes 1.5 seconds at slow walk.
- Meter at 0: the player sleeps, loses one wake-up, and wakes in the café.

### Wake-ups

- Start: 5. Maximum: 5.
- Switching off a city tower restores 1 wake-up, up to the maximum.

### Coffee supply and weapon costs

- Carry limit: 12 cups. Mobile café level 2 raises the limit to 20.
- Cup throw: 1 cup. Travel Mug: 0, it returns. Thermos: 3 cups per throw.
- Coffee Minigun: 6 cups per second. Moka Launcher: 4 cups per shot.
- Steam Wand, Tamper, Portafilter, Bean Slick, Iced Sprayer: no coffee cost.
- Caffeine cluster bomb: a crafted charge, not from the supply. Carry maximum 3.

### Combat feel

- Cup flight: 1 second, arc, wide aim cone.
- Dodge roll: 0.4 seconds, invulnerable during the roll.
- Steam Wand parry window: 0.3 seconds. A parried hug staggers the sleepwalker for 3 seconds.
- Hug grab range: short. A grabbed player mashes to break free.

### Brewing skill

- Every method has a skill from 0 to 10.
- Good brew (score 80 or more): +1 skill. Perfect brew (95 or more): +2.
- One master lesson: +2 skill. Every master teaches three lessons.
- Skill 10: auto-brew opens for the method. Auto-brew always scores 80. It never scores 95.
